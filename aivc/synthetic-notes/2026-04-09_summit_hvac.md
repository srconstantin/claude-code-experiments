drove down to greensboro w/ marcus, met w/ ron carlsen at the shop after lunch

backgrounds

- son tyler is the field supervisor, 34, "wants to take over but doesnt want to own it" — perfect setup for rollover equity + GM role
- ~74 employees, 31 trucks, 4 dispatchers. union? no, right to work state.

numbers (per ron, no QoE yet)

- ebitda: ~$3.6M (15-18%), they say $4.1M after addbacks
- addbacks include: ron's salary $480k (way above market — replace at $200k GM), wife on payroll $90k (doesnt come in), boat (yes really, $44k/yr), country club $22k, life insurance $61k

ron's ask: 5.5x adj ebitda. so $22M-ish enterprise value. plus the building (he owns the shop + 3.2 acres) at $1.8M, prefers sale-leaseback. mostly cash but open to 15% rollover.

competitive picture — local. 4 other guys his size in the triad area. he's the only one w plumbing license + HVAC under one roof for residential. some of the chain guys (one hour, ARS) are in the market but he says they cant touch his maintenance member base on price or quality

key risks
- ron IS the brand. his face is on the trucks. when he leaves we will lose customers, q is how many
- trade labor shortage — he has open req's for 6 techs right now, has had them for 4 months
- the plumbing line of business is newer (started 2019) and the margins are way thinner. need to break that out
- license transfer — NC requires a qualifier individual w master license. tyler doesnt have it. ron does. so deal is contingent on tyler getting qualified or hiring someone, this is real
- HVAC equipment supply chain has been crazy, watchout for inventory normalization in WC peg

thesis
this is a textbook lower-mm hvac platform. recurring maintenance base of 17k members is the moat. tyler is the right successor IF we keep him motivated w meaningful equity. 5.5x is full but defensible given the rec rev. could be the platform for 4-5 add-ons across NC/SC over 3 yrs

addback diligence is going to be a fight w ron, he's been running this thing like a personal piggy bank. real ebitda is probably $3.2-3.4M not $4.1M.
