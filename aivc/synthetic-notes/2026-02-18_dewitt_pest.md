# DEWITT PEST & TERMITE — first meeting

met at their office in mobile AL feb 18. drove down w/ jess.

owners: gary dewitt (62, founder) and dan dewitt (58, gary's younger brother). gary owns 60%, dan 40%. been in business together since 1989. they don't get along anymore which is why this is happening. gary wants to retire fully, dan wants to keep working maybe 5 more years.

so this isnt a clean two-fer. need to think about structure.

biz overview
- residential + commercial pest, also termite, also mosquito (seasonal)
- 7 service trucks + 2 termite trucks
- 22 employees, 14 are licensed pest control operators (PCO)
- 3 counties in coastal AL + western FL panhandle
- founded 1989, family before that was in agricultural supply

financials
- 2024 rev: $4.8M
- 2025 rev (preliminary): $5.2M
- ebitda: $1.95M (2025), ~37% margin which is consistent w good pest co's
- addbacks per gary: his comp ($340k vs $180 market for ops mgr) + a truck + cell phones for family members + the office building lease which they self-rent (need to normalize)
- recurring rev: 79% of total. ~9,400 residential plan customers + ~140 commercial accounts (restaurants mostly + some hotels in gulf shores)
- avg residential plan: $44/mo, annual churn ~9% (good)

very nice biz. clean recurring revenue, dense routes, good margins. textbook rollup target.

valuation
- they're asking 6.5x. that's high but pest is hot right now, big platforms (rentokil, terminix, aptive) are paying 7-9x for $5M+ ebitda assets, smaller stuff in this band trades 5-6.5x
- I'd come in at 5.5x ttm ebitda after normalized addbacks. that's $11M EV. plus probably a small earnout tied to '26 customer retention through transition.

team / transition
- gary out at close, maybe 6mo consulting agt
- dan stays as "regional president" for our platform, gets rollover equity (15%? we'll see)
- dan is the operational guy, gary is the rainmaker / commercial sales. when gary leaves who replaces commercial sales? need to identify or hire someone fast

risks
- the brother dynamic is messy. legal needs to look at the LLC operating agt carefully. if gary's exit triggers something w/ dan we have a problem.
- alabama PCO licensing: business needs a licensed responsible person on site. dan is the qualifier. if dan leaves before we've got our own, we're in trouble. need a 5 yr non-compete + employment agt that has real teeth.
- termite work has higher liability — they have a $2M umbrella, need to verify termite bond reserve adequacy
- commercial restaurant customers — high turnover among restaurants themselves, churn risk
- regional concentration. one hurricane and we lose service capacity for 6 wks. confirm BCP

upsides
- platform thesis: there are 12-15 small pest cos in southeast AL + panhandle FL we could roll up under dewitt as the platform brand. dan's relationships open doors.
- mosquito segment is growing 12-15%/yr nationally, they only do it casually rn
- commercial pricing is below market — they havent raised in 3 yrs. our platform model gets 4-6% price every year

i like this. its a real platform candidate not just a tuck-in. but the brother situation is going to require careful structure.
