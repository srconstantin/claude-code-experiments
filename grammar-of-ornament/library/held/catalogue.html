Catalogue of one form

Six prints of a single motif, cut from the drawing vgg16's features.17 makes of its channel 27. Each is printed in one ink from the feature's own colorway, on cotton rag, 40 × 40 cm.

The form

The catalogue's figure was cut by hand from one drawing; the network drew the same figure again elsewhere in the print, so each print is the first drawing, not a copy. The inks are the opponent pair of one ConvNeXt feature, each deepened until it holds against the paper.
