Ground

The wall behind this page is a single channel of a network trained on photographs, drawn until the channel fires as hard as it can: a cellular lattice in terracotta and pink, upscaled four times and joined at its edges so it runs on without a seam.

Everything else on the page takes its colours from the same feature: the paper is a tint of the biggest patch of colour in it, the ink and the accents are colours present in the picture.
