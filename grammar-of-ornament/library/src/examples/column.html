A channel deep in the network that answers to a bed of small round things in orange, broken by green: moss on a brick, roe, a fruit crate. Asked to draw it, the network draws this.

Here it is set as a column, the page's whole left side, at a size where its brushwork reads. The title runs down beside it, as a spine does. The text keeps to the right and stays narrow, so that the picture is never under it.

The colours on this page are the channel's own: the paper is a tint of its orange, the orange and the green of the type are pixels of it, the brown of the title's outline is what lies on the dark side of its edges.
