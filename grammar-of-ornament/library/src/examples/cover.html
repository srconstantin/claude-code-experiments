What a channel draws

Ask a network what one of its channels is looking for, and it draws a picture. Take that picture apart into the strokes it is made of, and the drawing is left: the hand of the network, in black.

This one, from the nineteenth layer of vgg16, draws leaves and seed heads, laid along one diagonal, each edge trimmed with small round marks. In colour it is green and violet. In line it is this, and it is as much the channel's signature as its colour was.
