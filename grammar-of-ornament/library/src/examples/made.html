Made of itself

A figure drawn with hundreds of copies of one small mark, the way a picture is built out of letters. The mark is a compound stroke from the same family of drawings; the figure is a motif cut from one print.

Nothing here is a texture. It is one shape, repeated, seen through another.

And in its own colours

The same motif, four times round a centre, filled with the interior of the print it was cut from. On the colorway's paper it reads as a rosette; on the dark side it glows.
